## Question 1

Airboats are used for transport across a river. To move the boat forward, air is propelled from the back of the boat by a fan blade.

An airboat has a fan blade of radius 1.8 m. This fan can propel air with a maximum speed relative to the boat of $20 \ \mathrm{ms}^{-1}$. The density of air is $1.2 \ \mathrm{kg/m^3}$.

**(a)** Outline why a force acts on the airboat due to the fan blade.

**(b)** In a test the airboat is tied to the river bank with a rope normal to the bank. The fan propels the air at its maximum speed. There is no wind.

**(i)** Show that a mass of about 240 kg of air moves through the fan every second.

**(ii)** Show that the tension in the rope is about 5 kN.

**(iii)** Show that the airboat moves away from the bank. The variation with time $t$ of the speed $v$ of the airboat is shown for the motion.

**(i)** Explain why the airboat has a maximum speed under these conditions.

**(ii)** Estimate the distance the airboat travels to reach its maximum speed.

**(iii)** Deduce the mass of the airboat.

## Question 6

An experiment to investigate simple harmonic motion consists of a mass oscillating at the end of a vertical spring.

The mass oscillates vertically above a motion sensor that measures the speed of the mass. Test 1 is carried out with a 1.0 kg mass and spring of spring constant $k_1$. Test 2 is a repeat of the experiment with a 4.0 kg mass and spring of spring constant $k_2$.

**(a)**

**(i)** Calculate the frequency of the oscillation for both tests.

**(ii)** Deduce $\frac{k_1}{k_2}$.

**(iii)** Determine the amplitude of oscillation for test 1.

**(iv)** In test 2, the maximum elastic potential energy $E_p$ stored in the spring is 44 J.

When $t = 0$ the value of $E_p$ for test 2 is zero.  
Sketch the variation with time of $E_p$ for test 2.

**(b)** The motion sensor operates by detecting the sound waves reflected from the base of the mass. The sensor compares the frequency detected with the frequency emitted when the signal returns.

The sound frequency emitted by the sensor is 35 kHz. The speed of sound is $340 \ \mathrm{ms^{-1}}$.

Determine the maximum frequency change detected by the sensor for test 2.
